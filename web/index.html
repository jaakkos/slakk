    2. Search area Band width, grid

    3. How steep Track, ground, kick-turns

    Skin track = how hard you climb along the line. Ground slope = how steep the hillside is under you (even on a traverse). Kick-turn = short steeper bursts. Ascent only — not a descent planner.
